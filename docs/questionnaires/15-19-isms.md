# CAS 15-19 — Information Security Management System Questionnaire

**Standard:** ISMS (ISO 27001)
**CAS Number:** CAS 15-19
**Bilangan Soalan:** 13
**Status:** Draft

**Nota:**
- Setiap soalan traceable ke PDF CAS 15-19
- `Guna: Kiraan` = input untuk formula-engine
- `Guna: Rekod` = simpan sahaja untuk auditor
- ISMS guna adjustment factors (ISO/IEC 27006 Table C.2, C.3, C.4)
- Q2-Q7: business + IT factor (skala 1-3) [PERLU SAHKAN]
- Formula-engine belum implement ISMS

## Section: Activities and Processes on Site

### Q1
- **Source:** CAS 15-19, Q1
- **Field:** `company_name`
- **Type:** `text`
- **Label:** Name of Company/Organisation
- **Required:** Yes
- **Applies To:** ISMS
- **Guna:** Rekod

### Q2
- **Source:** CAS 15-19, Q2
- **Field:** `critical_sector`
- **Type:** `radio`
- **Label:** Kindly indicate your organization type(s) of business and regulatory requirements
- **Description:** Critical business sectors are sectors that may affect critical public services that will cause risk to health, security, economy, image and government ability to function that may have a very large negative impact to the country
- **Required:** Yes
- **Applies To:** ISMS
- **Category:** Business Factor (C.2)
- **Guna:** Kiraan — Adjustment [PERLU SAHKAN]
- **Options:**
  - Organization works in non-critical business sectors and non-regulated sectors
  - Organization has customers in critical business sectors
  - Organization works in critical business sectors

### Q3
- **Source:** CAS 15-19, Q3
- **Field:** `process_complexity`
- **Type:** `radio`
- **Label:** Briefly describe your organization process and task
- **Required:** Yes
- **Applies To:** ISMS
- **Category:** Business Factor (C.2)
- **Guna:** Kiraan — Adjustment [PERLU SAHKAN]
- **Options:**
  - Standard processes with standard and repetitive tasks; lots of persons doing work under the organization's control carrying out the same tasks; few products or services
  - Standard but non-repetitive processes, with high number of products or services
  - Complex processes, high number of products and services, many business units included in the scope of certification

### Q4
- **Source:** CAS 15-19, Q4
- **Field:** `ms_level`
- **Type:** `radio`
- **Label:** To which extend level of establishment for the Information Security Management System implemented in your organization?
- **Required:** Yes
- **Applies To:** ISMS
- **Category:** Business Factor (C.2)
- **Guna:** Kiraan — Adjustment [PERLU SAHKAN]
- **Options:**
  - ISMS is already well established and/or other management systems are in place
  - Some elements of other management systems are implemented, others not
  - No other management system implemented at all, the ISMS is new and not established
